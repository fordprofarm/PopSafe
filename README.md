💥 Çözülen Problem Nedir?

Crystal PvP veya Anchor PvP atarken toteminiz patladığında saniyenin onda biri kadar sürede sol elinize (offhand) yeni bir totem çekmeniz gerekir. Fakat patlayan kristaller ve anchorlar etraftaki blokları (toprak, kırık taş, derin kayrak, netherrack, obsidyen vb.) kırar. Yerdeki bu çöp bloklar anında envanterinize dolar ve tam da totem çektiğiniz boş slotu işgal eder!

Panikle tekrar totem çekmek istediğinizde elinize taş parçası gelir ve sonraki patlamada ölürsünüz.

PopSafe, envanterinize giren istenmeyen blokları saliseler içinde otomatik olarak yere fırlatarak totem yuvalarınızın her zaman temiz kalmasını sağlar!

💥 The Problem PopSafe Solves

In Crystal PvP and Anchor PvP, popping a totem triggers a frantic refill: you immediately swap a fresh totem from your hotbar or inventory into your offhand.

However, crystal and respawn anchor explosions constantly shatter surrounding blocks (cobblestone, deepslate, netherrack, obsidian, basalt, dirt, tuff). These broken items get sucked into your inventory and instantly occupy your empty totem slot.

When panic-swapping, you accidentally grab useless cobblestone instead of a totem, leading to instant death on the next blast.

PopSafe eliminates this issue by automatically detecting and dropping unwanted junk blocks in milliseconds, keeping your totem refill slots clean at all times!

✨ Features
⚡ Instant Anti-Junk Dropper: Configured junk blocks are discarded the moment they enter your inventory.
🛡️ Full Inventory & Hotbar Modes: Protect your entire 27-slot inventory or hotbar only.
✋ Offhand & Armor Safe: Totems, shields, and equipped armor pieces are strictly protected and never dropped.
🔒 Container Safety: Automatically suspends dropping while viewing chests, ender chests, or anvils.
🚀 Anti-Kick Rate Limiting: Built-in packet throttle prevents server kicks from anti-cheats (GrimAC, Vulcan, NCP, 2b2t, etc.).
🌐 Bilingual (English & Turkish): Clean interactive language selector on first launch, switchable anytime.
⭐ Extras & Custom Item Manager:
Add any custom block ID (e.g., scaffolding, glowstone, glass).
Toggle custom blocks DROP or KEEP individually with full pagination.
On-Screen Totem Counter (HUD): Displays remaining totems with live color indicators.
Audio Feedback: Subtle click sound when items are purged.
⌨️ Default Keybinds
V : Open Settings & Extras GUI
H : In-Game Quick Toggle (Action-bar notification)

(All keybindings can be rebound in Minecraft's Controls menu).
